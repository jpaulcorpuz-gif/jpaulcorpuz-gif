Case study

Enrollment website and Stripe payments

The public side of the CAL ACE system: students pick a cohort, take a short English assessment, pay through Stripe and land on the roster, with no staff typing in between.

ClientCAL ACE Nursing Academy
RoleProduct owner / system designer
StackApps Script · Sheets · Stripe

01Registration

Students fill the form out once. It creates their student account, puts them on the roster for the start date they pick, and tells the Registrar which clinical group to hold for them.

02Live seats and waitlist

Every cohort shows a live seat counter, computed from the actual roster. When a cohort fills, the site moves new registrants to the waitlist automatically. Staff open, close and resize cohorts from the tracker’s Class Configuration tab, and the website follows instantly.

03Assessment and payment

  • A built-in English reading assessment before payment. Low scores are flagged for staff, not used to reject anyone.
  • Stripe payments for registration fees and tuition, including installment plans, with automatic receipts.
Pick a cohort→ English assessment→ Pay with Stripe→ Receipt sent→ On the roster

04After registering

Students log in to a dashboard to upload their valid photo ID, TB test and physical, check which requirements are still open, and see their balance.
